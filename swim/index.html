The problem with streaming

Bluetooth stops at the surface.

Radio signals barely travel through water, so a phone on the pool deck goes silent the moment your head goes under. Dipsana keeps the music on the headset instead: 32GB onboard, loaded once, played offline for the whole set.

  1. Above waterBluetooth 5.4 to your phone or watch.
  2. Under waterPlays straight from onboard storage. No signal needed.

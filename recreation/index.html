Where it goes

Anywhere you’d rather not miss a thing.

  1. Beach

    Sand stays out.

    Nothing sits in your ear canal, and IP68 means it’s dust-tight. Swim in the shallows with it on, then rinse it in fresh water.

  2. Pool

    Lengths or lounging.

    Waterproof to two metres, with music stored on the headset because Bluetooth doesn’t work underwater.

  3. Walk

    Podcast on, world on.

    Hear cars pulling out, cyclists behind you and the friend who just caught you up.

  4. Hike

    No signal? No problem.

    32GB onboard plays offline, so the album keeps going where your phone gives up. Ten hours covers a long day.

  5. Run

    Nothing bouncing in your ears.

    The band sits behind your head, there are no buds to shake loose, and you still hear footsteps and traffic.

  6. Ride

    Hear what’s coming.

    Open ears keep traffic and other riders audible. Check the local rules on headphones before you ride.

  7. Paddle

    If it goes in, fine.

    Kayak, paddleboard, rowing boat. A capsize is a wet headset, not a lost earbud at the bottom of the lake.

  8. Garden

    No bud lost in the soil.

    Weeding, mowing, washing the car. Sweat and hose spray are no problem, and the doorbell still gets through.

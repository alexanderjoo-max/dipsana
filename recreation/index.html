Where it goes

Anywhere you’d rather not miss a thing.

  1. Beach

    Sand stays out.

    IP68 is dust-tight as well as waterproof, and nothing sits in your ear canal. Rinse in fresh water after the sea.

  2. Pool

    Lengths or lounging.

    Waterproof to two metres, with music stored on the headset, because Bluetooth doesn’t reach underwater.

  3. Walk

    Podcast on, world on.

    Hear cars pulling out, cyclists behind you and the friend who’s just caught you up.

  4. Hike

    No signal, no problem.

    32GB onboard plays offline, so the album keeps going where your phone gives up. Ten hours covers a long day.

  5. Run

    Nothing bouncing in your ears.

    The band sits behind your head, so there are no buds to shake loose. You still hear footsteps and traffic.

  6. Ride

    Hear what’s coming.

    Open ears keep traffic and other riders audible. Check the local rules on headphones before you ride.

  7. Paddle

    If it goes in, fine.

    A capsize means a wet headset, not an earbud lost at the bottom of the lake.

  8. Golden hour

    The walk home.

    Ten hours of battery means there’s still music left when the sun goes down.

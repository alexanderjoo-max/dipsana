Why it feels different

Vibration, not volume.

A singing bowl works because you feel it as much as hear it. Dipsana does the same thing on purpose: two small pads rest on your cheekbones and pass sound through the bone to your inner ear.

Nothing goes in your ear canal, so there is no pressure, no seal and no muffled world. You stay aware of the room around you, and the sound settles underneath it.
